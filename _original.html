We exist so no one is left behind

Hardship can arrive suddenly: an illness, a lost job, a journey away from home. Help They Need was founded to give people practical support quickly, and to treat everyone with respect while we do it.

We work with local volunteers, schools, clinics and community leaders, so help reaches people where they live.

Dignity first

People choose the help that suits them. We never decide for them.

How we help

A simple process, with a real person at every step.

  1. Reach out

    Send a request online or through a local partner. It takes a few minutes.

  2. We listen

    A caseworker contacts you within two working days to understand your situation.

  3. Get support

    Together we agree a plan and arrange food, school costs, shelter or referrals.

  4. Stay connected

    We follow up until you are stable, and keep the door open afterwards.

Questions we often hear

Who can ask for help?

Anyone facing hardship, including older people, students, refugees and families. You can also ask on behalf of someone else.

Does it cost anything to get support?

No. Our services are free for the people we help.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. We only share details with partners when you agree, and only to arrange your support.

How is my donation used?

Most goes directly to programs. We publish an annual report showing every category of spending.

Can I volunteer from home?

Yes. Phone calls, online tutoring and translation can all be done remotely.
